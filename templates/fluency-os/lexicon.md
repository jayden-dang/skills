# Lexicon — chunks, not words

An entry is filed only when all four columns are real: the chunk as actually used, what it
*does* in a conversation, an authentic example with a source link, and one sentence the
learner wrote themselves about their own work or life.

A word without its partners does not become speech. Bare headwords are filed only when the
word genuinely is the whole unit.

States match the capability ladder: R1 recognised · R2 used with preparation · R3 used
unprompted. `next_due` follows `config.due_buckets`.

Live entries stay within `config.limits.lexicon_live`. Over cap, the **oldest R3** rows retire —
never the newest.

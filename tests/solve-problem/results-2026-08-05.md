## Observed failure

`gpt-5.6-terra` produced a useful brief but invented an unsupported success
threshold:

> “Reformulation rate: reduce from 18% to ≤14%”

It also instructed the engineer to turn the prototype into a decision comparison
within the imposed 20-minute deadline despite the absence of a relevance baseline
or representative evaluation set:

> “in the next 20 minutes, turn the prototype output into the above comparison table”

The brief omitted an explicit provenance/status field that would distinguish a
user-provided success criterion from a derived or unresolved one. It also lacked
an explicit `gap verdict` and reversibility/risk classification, leaving the next
route dependent on prose interpretation.

## Failure classification

- Primary: output has the wrong shape → positive recipe.
- Secondary: required elements omitted → REQUIRED slots.
- Conditional: if success criteria are not supplied or evidence-derived, preserve
  them as unresolved rather than inventing a number.

This result authorizes only text addressing these observed failures. The first
three scenarios do not authorize additional prohibitions or ceremony.

## GREEN iteration 1

Both models selected B and preserved the relevance target as unresolved. The
weakest model opened three new output-shape loopholes:

- Converted the observed p95 baseline into a guard threshold: “Search p95 remains
  at or below the current 90 ms baseline.”
- Assigned the prototype author as decision owner: “Decision owner: principal
  engineer.”
- Classified blast radius and reversibility without a stated basis.

These authorize explicit derivation rules for baseline-versus-threshold,
decision-owner provenance, and evidence-backed risk classification.

## REFACTOR iteration 2

Both models selected B and completed every required slot. Neither model invented
a target, guard threshold, decision owner, blast radius, or reversibility class.
Both explicitly treated the 20-minute deadline as a reporting constraint rather
than decision evidence and routed the unresolved decision to
`clarify-decisions`.

## Description routing sample

`frame-change`, `amend-feature`, and `reroute-plan`.

## Meta-test

The tested agent reported that the required shape, provenance, and unresolved
handling were clear. It identified route selection, prompt-fact timestamps, and
the meaning of first handoff input as remaining ambiguities. The skill now has a
closed class-to-route table, requires unresolved timestamps when none were
supplied, and separates available handoff evidence from missing evidence.
